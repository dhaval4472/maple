How the list is built

Fewer surprises at the counter

  • check_circle A part appears when its reorder level is above zero and the shelf count is at or below that level.
  • check_circle The suggested order is the restock target minus what is on hand, with a minimum of one.
  • check_circle Critical parts are pinned first so a stopped job is easy to spot.
  • check_circle Estimated cost uses the current unit cost and is a guide, not a supplier quote.
