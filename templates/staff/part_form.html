Simple by design

How stock stays honest

  • check The quantity on hand is only changed by a stock movement.
  • check Receiving a delivery raises the balance and can update the cost.
  • check Using a part lowers it and can be linked to a work ticket.
  • check A count or adjustment always records who did it and why.

lock Cost and stock value are only visible to shop owners. Employees can search, see quantities and record parts on their own jobs.
