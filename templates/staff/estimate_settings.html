Before you switch it on

A quick checklist

  • check At least one service has a base price and is switched on.
  • check The spread is wide enough to cover normal variation.
  • check The disclaimer still says it is not a quote.
  • check “Prices last reviewed” is set so visitors know the age of the numbers.

info MPI claims never show a shop range. The public page sends them to the claim navigator instead.

Multiplier maths

Worked example

Base $400, spread 20%, SUV ×1.15, moderate ×1.30, rounding to $5.

$400 × 0.80 × 1.15 × 1.30 = $478 Low end rounds down to $475.
$400 × 1.20 × 1.15 × 1.30 = $718 High end rounds up to $720.
