Privacy & photo policy

Plain language about your details.

This page explains what the website collects, why, and how long we keep it. It covers callback requests, booking requests, damage photos, review permissions and the first-party analytics we use to improve the site.

schedule

Default photo retention: {{ retention_days }} days. Ask us to remove photos sooner at any time.

What we collect

  • Callback and booking requests: your name, phone number, optional email, vehicle details, service, preferred time or window, and any notes you add.
  • Photo requests: the same contact details, a description of the damage, and the photos you choose to upload.
  • MPI claim navigator: the answers you provide and the contact details you choose to send.
  • Analytics: the page you viewed, the event name, your browser session and basic request information. We do not store the content of form fields in analytics.

Why we collect it

We use these details to reply to your request, prepare for a conversation, confirm a drop-off window and keep the shop's records. We do not sell your details. We do not add you to a marketing list from a repair request.

Damage photos

Photos are used to assess the request you sent. They are stored on the shop's website server and are not published unless you give separate written permission for a repair story. Sending a photo request for an estimate is not permission to publish the photo.

The default retention period is {{ retention_days }} days from the date of the request. If you want a photo removed sooner, call {{ phone_display }} or email {{ shop_email }} and tell us the reference number.

Review permissions

We publish individual customer comments only when we have them from a real customer. We do not invent reviews, and we do not publish an aggregate star rating until the source data is verified. If you want a comment removed, contact the shop and we will review it.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses a first-party analytics endpoint to record page views and interactions such as opening a callback form or completing a repair tool. The analytics record is tied to a browser session, not to an advertising profile. We do not currently load a third-party advertising pixel.

The contact page includes a Google Maps embed. Google may set its own cookies when that embed loads. If you prefer to avoid it, call the shop for directions instead.

How long we keep requests

Callback, claim and booking requests are kept while the shop follows up and for reasonable business records afterward. Photo requests follow the {{ retention_days }}-day default unless a shorter period is requested. Analytics events are kept for a limited reporting period and are not used to identify you.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to remove a request or photo. Call {{ phone_display }} or email {{ shop_email }}. We may need to confirm the request relates to you before changing a record.
