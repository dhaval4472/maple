Preferred drop-off

Pick a window. We confirm the details.

Tell us the vehicle, the service and a two-hour window that works. This is a request, not a confirmed appointment — the shop calls to confirm capacity and the plan.

info

Why a request? Repair timing depends on parts, inspections and the work already in the shop. A person confirms what is actually available.

What happens next

  1. 01
    Send the request.

    A person at the shop reviews the preferred window.

  2. 02
    We call to confirm.

    We check capacity, parts and whether an inspection is needed first.

  3. 03
    Bring the vehicle.

    You get the confirmed window and what to bring.
